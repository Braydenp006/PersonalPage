Privacy Policy

Bandits Custom Tables ("we") respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information this website collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, your email address, the service you're interested in, and your message. We don't ask for anything else.

Why we collect it

We use it only to reply to you about your enquiry: answering questions, discussing timing and pricing, and arranging your project. We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we won't add you to a mailing list without asking first.

Who handles it

Messages from the contact form are delivered to us by Formspree, a form service, which may store them on servers outside Canada. Apart from that, your information stays with us.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device's IP address when you visit, as is standard for any website using them.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry only as long as we need it to deal with your request and any project that follows, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted at any time. Just get in touch using the details below.
