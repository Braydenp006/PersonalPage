Local hardwoods, each with its own character

Most of our wood comes from trees felled right here in Ontario. These are the species we see most; ask about anything else.

Walnut

Deep chocolate browns with a creamy sapwood edge. The classic for a table that gets noticed.

Dining tables · desks · live edge

Maple

Pale and bright with a fine, tight grain. Hard-wearing and clean-looking.

Countertops · tables · shelves

Oak

Strong, open grain with a warm honey tone. Takes a stain beautifully.

Dining tables · benches

Ash

Light, with bold straight grain. Tough, and a little springy.

Consoles · benches · bases

Elm

Wild, interlocking grain full of movement. No two slabs alike.

Live edge · coffee tables
